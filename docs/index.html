How it works

Every 15 minutes Finad reads about 20 news sources, including CNBC, MarketWatch, WSJ, FT, NYT, BBC, Al Jazeera, the Federal Reserve and Google News, plus company news for every stock you hold or follow.

  1. ReadEach story is processed as it arrives. Watch it happen in the Live newsroom.
  2. ConnectFinad finds the companies a story names and its tone (beats, raised guidance, upgrades, or misses, probes, recalls). It also detects 30 world drivers, such as oil, rates, tariffs, war and AI spending, and the stocks each one helps or hurts.
  3. ScoreIt weighs every story by source and recency into a news impact for 190+ stocks, then crosses that with where each stock sits in its 52-week range.
  4. AlertA stock that newly turns SELL or BUY raises an alert on your desktop and on your phone, with the reasons and the headlines behind it.
SELLNear the 52-week high, and the news turns against it. Little upside is left, and the risk is skewed down.
BUYNear the 52-week low, and the news turns in its favour. It's beaten down, and a catalyst is building.
AVOIDNear the low, and the news is still negative: a falling knife.
HOLDNear the high, and the news still supports it: momentum intact.

Alerts on your Android phone

The Finad app for Android shows your signals, alerts, world drivers and the latest market-moving news, and notifies you of every new sell or buy alert. Link it once by scanning the QR code on Finad's Phone page. The app updates itself.

How the link works

After each update, Finad on your computer writes a compact feed to textdb.dev, a free public JSON store, under a secret key. Your phone gets a read-only link to that feed, so it can read but never change it, and checks it about every 15 minutes.

What is sent

Signals, alerts, world drivers, headlines, and which of your stocks are flagged. Your share counts and costs are never sent. Anyone with the read-only link can read the feed, so keep the QR code to yourself, and make a new link at any time.

Rules it follows

Runs on your computer

News, prices, analysis and AI all run locally. Data lives in one folder (%USERPROFILE%\.finad).

Public sources only

Public RSS feeds and Yahoo Finance prices. No account, no API keys, no tracking.

Updates you can check

The desktop and Android apps update from this website, and a download that doesn't match its published SHA-256 checksum is never installed.

Not financial advice

Finad's signals are automated readings of public headlines. It's a research tool, not a licensed adviser. Check the sources before you trade.
