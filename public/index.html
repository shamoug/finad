2 · Link your phone

How the phone link works

After each update, Finad writes a compact feed to textdb.dev, a free public JSON store with no account: the signals, your holdings' signals, alerts, world drivers and the latest market-moving headlines. It writes under a secret key that only this computer knows. The phone gets a read-only link to that feed, so it can read but never change it. The app checks the feed in the background about every 15 minutes and notifies you of new alerts. Anyone who has the read-only link can read the feed, so keep the QR code to yourself. Your share counts and costs are never sent.

About

Finad runs on this computer. It reads public RSS feeds and Yahoo Finance prices; with Ollama installed, a local model also reads the headlines. Your holdings stay here. Only the phone feed described under Phone leaves the computer, and only when you link a phone.

News sources
Finad produces automated, algorithmic signals from public news. It is not a licensed financial adviser, and its signals are not personalised investment advice. Markets can move against any signal. Check the sources and do your own research before you trade.
